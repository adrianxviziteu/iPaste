01How does iPaste open?+

Move your pointer to the top of the screen, or use the keyboard shortcut. Your shelf opens above the app you are already using.

02Does my clipboard leave my Mac?+

No. iPaste keeps your history on-device. No account, no server, no upload of your copied content.

03What can I save?+

Text, links, images, screenshots, code, colors, and files — organized automatically.

04Which Macs are supported?+

iPaste is built for Macs running macOS Sonoma 14 or later.

05Is it really free?+

Yes. iPaste is free forever and open source under the MIT license.
