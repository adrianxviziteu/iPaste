Privacy Policy.

Last updated August 20, 2026

The short version

iPaste is local-first. Your clipboard history — text, links, images, colors, code, and files — is stored only on your Mac. iPaste does not have a server, does not create an account for you, and does not upload your clipboard content anywhere.

What iPaste stores

iPaste keeps a history of items you copy so you can find and reuse them later. This history, along with any collections or snippets you create, is saved locally on your device using standard macOS storage. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

What we don't collect

  • We don't collect or see the contents of your clipboard.
  • We don't require an account, email, or sign-in to use the app.
  • We don't use analytics or tracking SDKs inside the app.
  • We don't sell or share data, because we don't collect any to begin with.

Permissions

iPaste may request macOS permissions (such as Accessibility or Screen Recording) that are required for clipboard monitoring and pasting to work system-wide. These permissions are used solely to power the app's core functionality on your device and are never used to transmit data off your Mac.

This website

This marketing website does not use cookies or trackers. If you email us at hello@ipaste.app, we use your message only to reply to you.

Open source

iPaste is MIT licensed. You're welcome to review the source code yourself to verify these claims at github.com/adrianxviziteu/iPaste ↗.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of iPaste after a change means you accept the revised policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach out at hello@ipaste.app.
